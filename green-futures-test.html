The four pillars

01
Environmental restoration

School land becomes living infrastructure — combating soil erosion and rebuilding what decades of deforestation have taken.

02
Economic empowerment

Seedling sales create a revenue stream that directly funds menstrual health for girls — no donor dependency required.

03
Capacity building

Students, teachers, and community members gain real horticultural and business skills — assets that outlast any single program cycle.

04
Self-sustaining cycle

Every profit is reinvested. The nursery funds welfare, welfare motivates stewardship, and stewardship grows the nursery — indefinitely.
